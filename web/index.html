    Data & methodology

    The Bank of Canada publishes business-day observations. The most recent date can differ across series. The policy rate and Bank Rate are shown as steps. A dash in the table means that no observation was published for that date and series.

    How closely did markets anticipate the next decision? Compare survey forecasts with announced targets in Policy Rate Scorecard.

    FX rates are Canadian dollars per unit of foreign currency. In indexed view, 100 is each currency’s first available observation within your chosen range. The data CSV gives the underlying published values.
